Flipper Zero ESP32 port for M5StickC Plus2

Web flasher - Chrome or Edge on desktop, USB cable.

This browser can't flash. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over https (GitHub Pages) to flash.

Plug the stick in, press the button, pick the serial port. Only the firmware is replaced - your apps, IR packs and saved files stay.

What's in this build

  • WiFi → App Store - install games from v1nx0/m5stick-games and trollmaster419/m5resources
  • Games menu - Snake + 11 games: Sky Hopper, Brick Breaker, Mines, Tic-Tac-Toe, Four in a Row, Merge Tiles, Pattern Memory, Reaction Test, Space Dodge, Star Shooter, Hi-Lo - each in its own colors
  • Infrared → Universal → AC - new On button next to Off, plus Hot / Cold
  • Infrared → Universal → AC Full Control - choose your brand (73 brands, 61 AC protocols), then On/Off, mode, temperature 16-30 and fan - or "Find my AC" tries every brand until yours reacts
  • Full TV & projector remotes - every button, in Infrared → Saved Remotes → TVs / Projectors (installed by WiFi → Update SD)
  • IR Tools - Sleep Timer, Clap Off (double clap = TV/AC off), Tilt Remote (volume / channel)
  • WiFi → Wake-on-LAN - turn on your PC over the network
  • Bigger IR remotes - WiFi → Update SD installs extra codes for TV, AC, audio, fans, LEDs and projectors (more brands, projector Play / Pause) - no App Store step needed
  • App Store extras - Ringtones player
  • Always-on top bar - clock and battery % on every screen, games included (like Bruce)
  • Israel time - clock runs on Jerusalem time (summer time automatic), keeps time while the stick is off (built-in clock chip) and corrects itself from the internet when WiFi connects

Buttons

  • A (front) = OK, hold = long OK
  • B (side) tap = Down, hold = Up
  • Power = Back

Based on trollmaster419/Flipper-Zero-ESP32-Port (port of Sor3nt/Flipper-Zero-ESP32-Port). AC protocols from IRremoteESP8266 (LGPL-2.1). Flash at your own risk.
